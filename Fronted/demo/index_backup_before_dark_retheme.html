TrackNet Quick Guide

Welcome to TrackNet! Here's a quick overview of every feature.

Dashboard

  • KPI Cards — Real-time stats: active cameras, vehicles detected, plates recognized, alerts, and average city speed.
  • Live Detections Feed — Streaming ANPR reads with plate numbers, locations, and confidence scores.
  • Interactive City Map — Procedural vector map with camera markers, vehicle positions, and heat zones. Use the layer buttons (Cameras, Vehicles, Heat zones) to toggle visibility.

Live Cameras

  • Camera Grid — 12 simulated CCTV streams with live ANPR overlays. Click any feed to inspect it.
  • Camera Status — Each feed shows online/offline status, detection rate, and ANPR status badges.

Vehicle Intelligence

  • Plate Search — Type any vehicle registration number to track its city-wide trajectory.
  • Journey Reconstruction — The map draws the vehicle's path across detected camera locations with timestamps.

Traffic Analytics

  • Hourly Trend Charts — Visualize traffic density patterns across time.
  • Zone Analysis — Traffic zones with heat intensity, origin-destination flows, and camera coverage.

Alerts

  • Alert Feed — Real-time alerts for blacklisted vehicles, flagged plates, and offline cameras.
  • Priority Filters — Filter by High, Medium, or Low priority using the filter chips.

Camera Network

  • Network Table — Complete inventory of all 256 cameras with status, location, and performance metrics.
  • Camera Details — Click any camera to see its live feed, detection stats, and maintenance info.

Settings

  • Simulation Controls — Toggle live traffic simulation, ambient particles, automatic alerts, and camera feed engine.
  • Platform Status — Engine version, uptime, cameras tracked, and map tile status.

Sidebar Controls

  • Collapse/Expand — Click the sidebar toggle button at the bottom to shrink into icon-only mode or expand back.
  • Dark/Light Mode — Toggle between light and dark themes using the moon/sun button.
  • This Guide — Click the help button anytime to reopen this guide.
